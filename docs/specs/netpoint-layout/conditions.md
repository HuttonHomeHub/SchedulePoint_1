# NetPoint layout — falsification conditions

- **Status:** Approved — committed alone before any measurement (ADR-0128 ordering); not yet built
- **Spec:** [`./feature-spec.md`](./feature-spec.md) §5, transcribed verbatim below
- **Committed:** 2026-09-23, before M0-T2 took a single reading

**These bars are fixed by this commit.** A later measurement may not move one; a condition that
turns out to be wrong is amended here in a dated entry that says what was wrong and quotes this
commit's hash, never edited in place. Every later write-up quotes the hash of this file's first
commit.

**CQ-1, the row budget, as approved (2026-09-23):** `B = seedRows + max(4, ⌈0.5 × seedRows⌉)`,
where `seedRows` is the row count of the layout a search starts from (Unit 300: 21 → 32). Travel
(Σ |Δlane| over links) is the tiebreak after same-row chains and before rows. FC-N10's frontier is
shown to the product owner **before M4 is built**, and B may move on those numbers — as a dated
amendment here, not an edit.

Every condition names its instrument, its bar, and what happens if it fails. Figures marked **M0**
are formulas whose denominators M0 measures; the formula is committed first and cannot be adjusted
afterwards (FC-L4's pattern).

**Yardstick plans:** `chain-3-placed` (new, §0.10), `small-17` (`small-plan-fixture.ts`), Unit 300
(`p6_torture_test_v1.xer`, 144 / 188), `scale-2000` (`scaleScene(2000)`). Zooms {1, 4, 12} px/day,
pans {0, 32, 200, 500} (`measure-occlusion.mjs:37-49`). Pitch 52 **and** 60 at M0.
