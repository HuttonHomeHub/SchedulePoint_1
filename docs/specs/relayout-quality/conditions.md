# Re-layout quality — falsification conditions

- **Status:** Approved — committed alone before any measurement (ADR-0128 ordering); not yet built
- **Spec:** [`./spec.md`](./spec.md) §1 "Success criteria", transcribed verbatim below
- **Committed:** 2026-10-17, before M0-T2 took a single reading

**These bars are fixed by this commit.** A later measurement may not move one; a condition that
turns out to be wrong is amended here in a dated entry that says what was wrong and quotes this
commit's hash, never edited in place. Every later write-up quotes the hash of this file's first
commit.

**Open questions, as the product owner answered them (2026-10-17):**

- **CQ-1:** Re-layout may use as many rows as the diagram has now, `max(current rows, pack rows)`.
  Tidy's budget is unchanged.
- **CQ-2:** up to four parallel starts, allowed only if Re-layout's wait at 300 activities is at most
  1.5× today's in the container (FC-R6). Otherwise extra starts run only below a measured size, and
  the pack start always runs.
- **CQ-3:** the critical path on one row is reported, not ranked.

**Defaults:** bends are reported, not ranked. No `VITE_*` flag (ADR-0088 D1). No new CI job or root
`check:*` gate. The corpus is the catalogue's `--tier all` plus generated `scale-300`. Text widths come
from the product's own `textWidthTable` in headless Chromium with the repo's woff2 loaded. Tidy is out
of scope and stays byte-identical.

The objective is ADR-0152's, unchanged in terms and order, lower always better:
`overlaps → links behind a bar (occluded) → crossings → unlinked contacts → −same-row links → travel → rows`.
