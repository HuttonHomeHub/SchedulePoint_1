What leaving the deck bought

Removing the two mode pairs and Recalculate took the deck from 35 items to 33, but the saving is larger than that sounds: the mode segments were the widest objects in the View group, and their removal collapses View from ten items to eight.

The deck is now four groups on two rows at 1646 px, and folding the two you use least gets it to one.

The staleness prompt

Worth being precise about, because it changes behaviour rather than appearance. Today Recalculate is a button you may press at any time, and nothing tells you whether you need to.

Now the plan asserts its own state: current reads quietly in green, stale names how many edits are outstanding and offers the fix. The command has not been removed — it has been attached to the condition it answers.
