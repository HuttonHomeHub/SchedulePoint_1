Selecting a flagged activity now says why it is flagged, in words, on the selection bar, in both the Diagram and the Gantt: "Constraint not met", "Placed before its logic allows", "Placed after its constraint date" or "Can't be levelled within its window", with every reason named when there are two. The line sits above the controls, so none of them moves. The button that answers the conflict also describes it to a screen reader, the conflict messages read the same wherever they appear, and the Gantt row menu no longer lists a "Fix this conflict" item that did nothing.
