On windows narrower than 768 px the plan workspace no longer swaps between a diagram view and an activities view. The diagram and the activities panel now behave as they do on a larger window: the panel opens with the Expand button at the foot of the workspace, and the plan's facts and the Recalculate button wrap onto their own lines instead of running off the edge. The side panels (Health check, Float paths, Compare revisions and Notes) can no longer squeeze the diagram to nothing: on a narrow window an open panel takes the whole row and the diagram waits behind it until you close it, or press Fit. The toolbar's Compare revisions control now opens its panel on narrow windows, where it previously showed nothing.
