A plan left open and untouched for 30 minutes now becomes free for colleagues to edit. Nothing pops up when it does: move the mouse or press a key and you carry on, unless a colleague started editing in the meantime, in which case you are told who. If a change you made at the very moment you came back could not be saved, you are told and can simply do it again. A form with changes you have typed but not saved keeps the plan reserved for you until you save or close it.
