The plan command deck now has one rule for when a control shows its text label, declared on the control. Baseline overlay, Resource view, Comments and Settings… are icon-only at every width; each keeps its accessible name and gains a tooltip saying what it does (shown on hover and focus, and on a long press with a finger), and Resource view takes a stacked-columns icon in place of the people glyph. Apply levelled dates… shows its label with a mouse once the deck is at least 79 rem wide (about a 1280 px window). Nothing else moves.
