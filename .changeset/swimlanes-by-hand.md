Plans can now have swimlanes: labelled bands across the diagram, such as one for each trade or area. Open View, expand Structure and choose Set up swimlanes… to name them. To put activities in a swimlane, select them and choose Move to swimlane…, or drag a bar into another swimlane. Rename, reorder or delete a swimlane from the menu on its name in the name column. Arrange works inside each swimlane, so the diagram can get taller. Exports, prints and the minimap show the swimlanes too, and the list for screen readers is grouped by swimlane. Turning the Swimlanes checkbox off puts every bar back exactly where it was, and turning it on again brings the bands back. If the swimlanes can't be loaded, the diagram says so and offers Try again. Adding, renaming, reordering, deleting and moving work between swimlanes can all be undone.
