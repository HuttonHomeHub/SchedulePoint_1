The Gantt has a Swimlane column when the plan has swimlanes. It shows each activity's swimlane, with a dash for none. Double-click a cell, then choose a swimlane from the list, to move that one activity there; the bar keeps its dates and Undo puts it back. While the swimlanes follow the WBS, or without the editing pen, the cell is read-only and says why. View, Columns lists the column and its width, and the printed programme leaves it out.
