On a short window, expanding the activities panel now shows its rows. At 1024 × 600 the panel used to open to little more than its heading and column titles. When the window is too short to show both the diagram and about three rows, Expand now gives the panel the whole workspace and hides the diagram; Collapse brings it back exactly as you left it, with the same position, zoom and selection. While the diagram is hidden, a toolbar command aimed at it (zoom, fit, go to date, the drawing tools, the side panels) first collapses the panel and then runs, a drawing tool that was armed is put away, and the panel says so. Opening a side panel such as Health collapses the activities panel, and expanding the panel closes the side panel. The panel's smallest height also rises from 140 to 245 pixels, so it always shows at least one row. The Settings… button now shows a gear instead of a calendar. Nothing changes on larger screens.
