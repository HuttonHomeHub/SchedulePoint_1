Make the plan's command bar one line that scrolls sideways in a window narrower than 1024 pixels, so a small window keeps room for the diagram and the foot row. A fade at the edge and a half-clipped control show that the line goes on, and keyboard focus scrolls the focused control into view clear of the edge. In a narrow window that is also short (26 rem or less, which includes a 1280 × 800 window at 200 % text size) the header and bar scroll away with the page. "Expand activities panel" and "Recalculate" can now be reached by touch at every small window size, and in a very narrow window the plan's name, status and Edit plan details drop under the logo instead of running off the screen. Windows 1024 wide and up are unchanged, including short ones such as 1280 × 600.
