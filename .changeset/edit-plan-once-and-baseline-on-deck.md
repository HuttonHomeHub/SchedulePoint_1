**Edit plan** is no longer offered twice. It sat both on the plan header's pencil and as a labelled
shortcut inside the Summary menu, wired to the same callback — same permission, same dialog, same
plan. The pencil stays.

**Baseline overlay** moves out of the View menu onto the command deck, so it is one press rather
than two. Measured: it costs nothing at 1920 or 1646, and adds a line to the deck at 1440.

Two of the three toggles originally chosen for promotion turned out not to be promotable — Float
paths is already on the deck, and Critical path is not a view toggle at all.
