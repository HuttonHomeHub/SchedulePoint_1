Swimlanes can now follow the WBS. Set up swimlanes offers "One per WBS group at level N" beside "By hand", preselected to the shallowest level with two groups. Each summary at that level becomes a swimlane named after it, and everything filed under it, at any depth, is in it. Swimlanes... lets you switch between by hand and the WBS as one undo step, and switching back returns the hand-made swimlanes exactly as they were. While the WBS leads, the activity editor's Swimlane field shows where the activity is and says why it can't be changed there.
