SchedulePoint now says plainly that it is designed for screens at least 1024 pixels wide. If you open the app, or move to another page, in a narrower window, you see a "designed for larger screens" page with tips (zoom out, widen the window, turn a tablet sideways) and who you are signed in as. If your window gets narrower while you are working, a slim strip appears at the top instead, and nothing you are typing is interrupted. Pressing "Continue anyway" once is what makes a half-screen window quiet on that device: the page and the strip never come back there. Escape or "Dismiss" only hides them for this visit.
