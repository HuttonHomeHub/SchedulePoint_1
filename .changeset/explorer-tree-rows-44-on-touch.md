On a touchscreen, the rows in the Project Explorer tree are now 44 px tall, with a 44 px "⋯" button, so they are easier to tap. With a mouse nothing changes. When you attach or detach a keyboard cover the tree keeps the row you were looking at, and keeps focus on the row you were on. The tree shows fewer rows at once on a touch device as a result.
