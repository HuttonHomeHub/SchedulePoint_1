The organisation landing's sections are re-ordered so that more of what a planner arrives for is
visible without scrolling: **Jump back in → Needs your attention → Where the work stands → Recently
changed**.

The order is measured rather than preferred. Each section's height was taken at 1646 px (158 / 463 /
789 / 749, on a 2,386 px page against a 1,000 px fold) and all 24 orderings were scored against the
position of each answer within its own section. **Four of seven above the fold is the ceiling**, and
this is the ordering that reaches it while still leading with the reader's own work.

The question that gains most is "is anything waiting on me?", which moves from **53 px below the
fold to 420 px above it** — the specific defect the measurement pass opened on. A plan's finish and
its movement against baseline come above the fold for the first time. The activity feed goes below,
knowingly: that is the trade four-of-seven is made of.

A two-column layout was measured and **not built**. Two columns wide enough to leave every existing
section at least as wide as it is today need 1,716 px of content box, and the target width is 1,646 —
so every existing section would have had to span both columns, which is a single column wearing a
grid's clothes.
