With two or more activities selected, the bar under the diagram now reads "3 selected" on one line (it wraps only when the window is too narrow), followed by Link in sequence…, Move to swimlane… and Set code… where they apply, Delete and Clear selection. The long sentence and the drag hint are no longer on screen: the sentence is read out as the bar's description, and the hint appears when you hover "3 selected" and is still spoken when the selection becomes plural. The bar costs the diagram less height than the single-activity bar does. Link in sequence… now stays available when the activities are already linked: it tells you "These 3 activities are already linked in this order. Nothing to add." and creates nothing, and when only some pairs are linked it adds just the missing links and says how many already existed. Clicking the bar that is already the only selection (the second click of a double-click) no longer announces it again.
