One empty-state vocabulary across the app. Thirty-four hand-rolled dashed boxes were counted;
seven were the wrong shape entirely — three failed requests that read as "nothing found", two
permission refusals wearing an absence's costume — and are now the error and refusal shapes.
The rest converge on `EmptyState` (tables and pages), `NoticeStrip emphasis="dashed"` (panels and
the canvas dock) or stay as prose where nothing is actually missing. Resource lists show a
column-matched loading skeleton instead of a centred spinner, and a filtered table now offers a
way back to the unfiltered one. A structural gate fails on the next hand-rolled box.
