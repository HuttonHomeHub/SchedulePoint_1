A read-only cell in the Gantt grid now shows why it cannot be edited when you try to edit it. Double-click or double-tap the cell, or press F2 on a row where nothing can be changed, and a tip gives the reason; a screen reader is told once on F2. Hovering shows the same tip, so the browser's own hover text is gone. Selecting a row with a single click shows nothing.
