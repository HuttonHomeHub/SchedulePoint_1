When a control is shaded because you cannot use it right now, the reason is now shown to everyone, not just to screen readers or to people hovering with a mouse. In a row menu the reason is printed under the item's name. On the toolbar, in the status bar's Recalculate button and in the selection bar's Isolate button, the reason appears as a tip when you hover over the control, tab to it, or press it, so it also reaches touch and keyboard users.
