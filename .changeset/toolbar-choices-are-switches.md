Choices on the plan toolbar now look like what they are. The Colour options and the Diagram | Gantt choice sit in a recessed track with the chosen one raised, and the chosen one stays clearly different in Windows high-contrast mode, where it used to look the same as the others. Undo, Redo and Recent edits are now one joined unit, in that order, so the small arrow no longer sits between Undo and Redo, and the Link "Type" options are joined too. The search box keeps an outline but a quieter one. Every button keeps its name, its size and its place in the keyboard order; Redo now comes before Recent edits when you arrow across them, matching what you see.
