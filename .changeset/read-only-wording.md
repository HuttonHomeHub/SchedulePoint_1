Clearer wording for people who cannot edit a plan. An empty plan now tells a Viewer or Contributor to
ask a Planner to add activities, instead of only saying their role cannot. When someone is editing a
plan, the selection bar names them and points to Request control; for a Planner who is not editing it
says to start editing. The words "edit lock" are gone from the screen: messages now say "editing"
instead, in the activity editor's unsaved-changes warning, the Make milestone dialog, the Members
role list, the organisation overview and the programme recalculation message.
