A plan that already has bars you placed by hand before their logic allows now says so in a message at the foot of the diagram and the Gantt: "3 bars stay where they were placed, before their logic allows." Press "Follow logic" to move all of them to where their logic puts them in one step (one Undo puts them all back), or "Dismiss" to hide the message for those bars for this session (it asks again after you reload the page, and about any other bar that later ends up in the same state). The count leaves out summary activities and activities that have started, so it can be lower than the number of conflicts shown in the toolbar. Choosing "Keep it here" on the message that follows an edit stops the plan message asking about that bar, and undoing "Follow logic" does not make it ask again. If more than 2,000 bars qualify, "Follow logic" is shaded with the reason.
