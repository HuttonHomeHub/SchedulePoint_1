The plan workspace now fits better on a laptop-sized window. At 1024 pixels wide the top bar is one row instead of two, which gives the diagram 48 pixels of height back, and a wide sidebar (the Project Explorer) can no longer squeeze the diagram below 720 pixels (the width you chose is kept and returns when the window widens). The "Search or filter" box is narrower below 1600 pixels, with a shorter hint, which lets the toolbar rows fit in two lines at 1280 pixels instead of three. Nothing changes on larger screens.

A correction to the 0.178.0 note: the "designed for larger screens" page's tip reads "Turn your device sideways", not "turn a tablet sideways", and the page also has a "Not now" button that hides it for this visit only.
