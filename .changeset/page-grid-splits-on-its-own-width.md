The organisation landing, the Members page and the staff console now show their boxes in two columns only when the page has the room, instead of whenever the window is wider than a tablet. With the Project Explorer open, a window narrower than about 1477 pixels now shows the landing and Members as one column of full-width boxes that you scroll down, where before it squeezed them into two narrow columns that cut off plan names and project names. This includes a 1440-pixel laptop, which now sees one wide column. Hiding the Explorer gives the room back and the two columns return, and widening the Explorer, or raising your text size, can turn two columns into one. Screens 1477 pixels wide or more, including a full-size monitor, look exactly as before. The staff console, which has no Explorer, changes only on windows narrower than about 1200 pixels.
