In the Gantt, a mouse press on the left edge of a row's three-dots menu or on the Float left heading no longer grabs the divider between the table and the chart instead. The three dots now sit inside the table, with a small empty margin at its right edge, so nothing in the table lies under the divider's grab area. The table is 44 px wider by default; the Activity column keeps its width.
