Zoom out, Zoom in, Fit to plan and the Minimap toggle move from the command deck to a small "Diagram viewport" toolbar in the diagram's bottom-right corner, with the minimap stacked above it so opening the minimap never moves the button under the pointer. The Minimap leaves View ▾. In the Gantt the cluster is absent, and focus on it moves to the Gantt segment of the view switch. When the stage is too short or too narrow for the minimap (a docked panel, or a selection bar on a touch screen) the Minimap button is shaded with "Not enough room for the minimap" and the cluster stays. A keyboard move onto an activity now keeps it clear of the cluster and the minimap. At 1024 × 600 with a mouse the command deck is two lines in every state. The "Conflict n of m" read-out on the deck now shows the count only; the reason is still announced to screen readers on every step.

Voice and assistive-technology commands are unchanged ("Zoom in", "Zoom out", "Fit to plan", "Minimap"), but the Minimap is now a button with a pressed state rather than a checkbox in View ▾.
