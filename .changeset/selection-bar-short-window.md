On a short window (up to 704 pixels high, such as a 1024 × 600 laptop), selecting an activity now gives its action bar the whole bottom row. The plan's data date, finish, activity count and the "Editing" badge step aside while the bar is there and come back as soon as you clear the selection, so the bar wraps onto fewer lines and the diagram keeps more height. Every action stays on screen with its label, and the hidden facts are still read out by a screen reader. Clicking a bar on the diagram no longer slides it when it is still fully in view, and when the bottom row covers part of it, it moves only as far as it needs to; moving through activities with the keyboard keeps its margin.
