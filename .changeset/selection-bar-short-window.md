On a short window (about 700 pixels high or less at normal text size), selecting one activity or several now gives the action buttons the whole bottom row. A "Locked" warning stays on screen, and a screen reader still reads the hidden facts. Clicking a bar on the diagram no longer slides it when it is still fully in view.
