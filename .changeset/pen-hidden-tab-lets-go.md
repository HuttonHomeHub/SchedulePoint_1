A plan left open in a background tab for 15 minutes stops holding other people out: within about two more minutes a colleague can take it over. Coming back to the tab, you carry on editing if nobody else started, and are told if someone did. Your undo history is kept, and any step that can no longer be applied is set aside with an explanation. A tab you leave in front, unattended, still holds the plan until you press Stop editing.
