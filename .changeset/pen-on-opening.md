When you open a plan that nobody else is editing, you can now edit it straight away: there is no Start editing button to press first. If someone else is already editing, nothing changes: you see who it is and can ask for control. Pressing Stop editing is respected until you open the plan again, and a plan you keep open in a second tab no longer tells you that someone took over when you close the other tab. Viewers and Contributors never take the pen.
