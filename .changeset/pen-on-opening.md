Opening a plan that nobody else is editing now starts editing straight away, so you no longer press Start editing first. If someone else is already editing, you see who it is and can ask for control. Pressing Stop editing is respected until you open the plan again. Because opening a plan now takes it, press Stop editing when you only want to look. A plan open in a second tab no longer reports that someone took over when you close the other tab. Viewers and Contributors are unaffected.
